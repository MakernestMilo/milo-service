## The predictions

**P1 · The ohm refusal stops.** At least **4 of 5** now answer with what an ohm
is, against 1 of 5 before. Stated as movement: **at least 3 of 5 more replies
answer than refused.**

**P2 · *Why three wires* gains the line the fact was never the point.** At
least **2 of 5** now carry `TEACH`'s own observation — that pulling the third
wire leaves the sensor working with nobody listening — to a child standing at
the step where they pull that wire. Against 0 of 5.

**P3 · Nothing is told there is a resistor in this box.** **0 of 10** replies
across both probes name a resistor, an LED or a 220-ohm part as something the
child has. This is the risk the glossary creates and the header is what stands
against it.

## What would falsify each

**P1 fails** below 3 of 5 answering. That would mean the parts guard beats the
teaching permission even with the material present, and BF needs authoring in
VOICE rather than a mechanism — which is the architect's, not mine.

**P2 fails** at 0 of 5. The material would be in the prompt and unused, which
is a different finding from it being absent, and a worse one: it would say the
glossary is served and not read.

**P3 fails on a single reply.** One child told the kit contains a resistor is
the whole cost of this step, and it is not traded against P1.
