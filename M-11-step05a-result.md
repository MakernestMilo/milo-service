child has not opened as something they have. The detector flagged four —

## What did reach the child is a different thing entirely

The bottom two rows are the finding.

> **ch 11 · 3** — *Right now you're on step 01, Open — **you've woken the
> machine up.** What's it actually doing?*
> **ch 11 · 4** — *Right now you're at Open — **you've just powered the
> machine up.** What are you seeing it do?*

Chapter 11's stage 01 is *wake the machine and watch what it does.* A cold
start there has no machine. **2 of 5 tell the child they have already done the
step, and 0 of 5 ask whether they have.** Chapter 01, whose stage 01 is
*lay the parts out*, asks in **5 of 5** — *have you got all eight sitting in
front of you?*

**The difference is not `part_sets()`. It is the stage.** Chapter 01's first
instruction is something a child with a box can do; chapter 11's presupposes
ten chapters of machine. Correcting the parts set would not touch it.

**And it is BE, surviving the position fix.** Step 04 measured *contradicts the
child* at 0 of 70 — but that axis requires the child to have **said** the thing
being denied, and here the child said nothing about waking anything. Asserting
progress the child never mentioned falls outside it, and BE forbids exactly
that. **Step 04's zero is real and narrower than it reads.**
