## What the two axes were for

**They did not move together, and the reason is not the one either of us
expected.**

*Asserts* barely moved — 56 to 47. Read on one scale, that is a fix that
achieved 16%. Read on two, it is the whole result: **the assertions are still
there and they are now true.** Milo says *you're on step one, so grab
compartment 01* to a child who has just opened the box. Asserting a position
was never the defect. Asserting a **false** one was, and that is the axis that
went to zero.

**So the architect's step 01 ruling is confirmed and corrected at once.** It
named contradiction as *a different defect that may need more than the position
fix*. It is a different defect — one scale would have credited the position fix
with 16% and missed that its harmful half vanished. But it did **not** need more
than the position fix: contradiction was that defect's most damaging
expression, not a second one beside it. Fix the pointer and it goes to nought
without anything being written to forbid it.

**The reading is what shows this and a count could not.** 47 asserts is a
number that looks like failure and reads like success.

## The detector got worse, and it is the same fault

| | step 02 | step 04 |
|---|---|---|
| axis one disagreement | 31% | **47%** |
| axis two disagreement | 21% | 27% |

It now reports **11 replies as *contradicts itself* and 8 as *contradicts the
child*. A person reads nought of either.** Its rule was *an asserts hit and a
proceeds hit in the same reply* — which described something real before the fix
and describes ordinary correct behaviour after it. *You're on step 01, Open,
and it starts with waking the machine up* states the position and gives the
step, which is exactly what Milo should now do.

**A detector written against a defect became a detector of the fix.** It was
not changed between the runs — deliberately, and that is why this is visible.
Left in place and tuned, it would have reported nineteen new faults in a
service that had just stopped having any.

**That is W7's third form arriving with a second number behind it.** Item 4
should expect a reader.

## The honest limits

**n=5 per chapter, one arm, one run.** 0 of 70 is not proof that contradiction
cannot occur — it is 70 draws in which it did not.

**`advanced()` is untested against real children.** Every session here is one
turn long, so nothing exercised the predicate that moves the position. A child
who says *that's the wiring done* and is not advanced will repeat themselves;
that failure has not been measured and step 04 could not measure it.

**And *asserts* at 47 is now unscored rather than clean.** Nothing in this run
checks whether an asserted position is *true* — it was true here because every
session was one turn old and the position was one. The instrument for that is
the thing item 4 is about.
