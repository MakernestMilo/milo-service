# The L3 route split — hypothesis, and the test that would settle it

Written before the second five of `eleven` runs.

## The observation, from one five

Chapter 11, L3, thirty calls. Same rung, same rung material, **one line of
difference in the prompt**: the override line, appended when `direct_asks` is
non-zero.

| route | premise rate | shape |
|---|---|---|
| L3 by direct ask | **0/5** | *"I don't know which of the five it is — nobody told me, so I can't guess."* Four of five decline outright. |
| L3 by clock | **3/5** | *"That's the sensor test."* |

## Hypothesis

The override line is the cause. Milo treats **an explicit request as a warrant to
be careful**, and a **clock arrival as an ordinary turn** where the gap wants
filling.

Stated as a hypothesis because one five is not a rate, and because the two
routes differ in one *observable* — nothing establishes that the observable is
the mechanism.

## What would falsify it, as a movement rather than a rate

Baselines move in this project, so the test is the **gap**, not either number.

- **Replicated** if the second five shows the same direction with a gap of
  **≥ 2 of 5** — clock above ask.
- **Not supported** if the gap collapses to **≤ 1 of 5**, in either direction.
- **Reversed** if ask exceeds clock by ≥ 2, which would mean the line is doing
  something other than what this hypothesis says.

## The test that would actually settle it, and why it is not this run

Observation cannot separate *the override line* from *having been asked*, because
in the corpus they always co-occur. Settling it requires an **intervention**: an
arm that serves the override line at L3-by-clock, where nobody asked.

That changes the prompt, so it is a fourth arm and a separate ruling — and it is
the shape of the guard-separation factorial from M-07, one field over. Named
here so the observational result is not read as the causal one.

## What is already true regardless of mechanism

A child who waits twenty-two minutes gets a worse answer than a child who asks.
That is measured, at one five, and it is the rung sheet 4 wrote the clock for.
