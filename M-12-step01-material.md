## The measurement you asked for, and it changes what can be authored

**Placing by parts and ports has a ceiling of seven.** Across the fourteen
chapters there are only **seven distinct board states**, because seven
chapters add neither a part nor a port to the one before them.

| board | parts | ports | chapters that share it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 | 4 | **01** |
| 2 | 10 | 6 | **02** |
| 3 | 11 | 7 | **03 · 04** |
| 4 | 12 | 8 | **05** |
| 5 | 15 | 9 | **06 · 07** |
| 6 | 16 | 10 | **08 · D · 09** |
| 7 | 18 | 10 | **10 · 11 · 12 · G** |

**The worst is four chapters on one board**: The Creature, Sabotage, Your Own
Machine and The Gift are identical in parts and ports — 18 and 10. A child at
The Gift, handing a finished machine to their grandad, has a board
indistinguishable from a child who has just clipped a card body on.

**BL's bound cannot place seven of fourteen.** What separates the collisions is
not parts but **what has been done** — a chart card with a week of dots on it,
a machine screwed to a door, a body made of card, a machine that has been
given away. BL excludes the stages by name, so this is a real limit on the
ruling rather than a gap in the material.

**It is not necessarily a defect.** Placing to a board state rather than to a
chapter may be enough: a child on the 18-part board is at 10, 11, 12 or G, and
Milo can ask one question to separate them. **But that is a second turn, and
BJ's ruling is one.** Stated here because you asked to know before authoring
rather than after.

## The constraint, and it is the openers' constraint again

**A description must not satisfy `matched()`.** BM says a placing turn does
not start the failure clock, and a description of a machine is richer than an
opener and more likely to collide — `NEG` catches *blank*, *dead*, *stuck*,
*frozen*, *nothing happens*, *no number*, and a child describing a board that
is not running will reach for those words honestly.

```bash
python3 tools/check_opener.py "the sentence"
```

The same check the fourteen openers took. It reports which chapters' clocks a
sentence starts and which of `matched()`'s two tests fired.

## What the third case has to do

**Fail to place, rather than place vaguely.** *There's a green thing and a
screen* places at chapter 01 without difficulty. The utterance that is needed
leaves Milo unable to choose, and the measurement is **whether Milo teaches or
asks again** — X6, and BK's case, with `TEACH` as the material and served
since M-11.

The failure mode it exists to catch is a loop: Milo asks what is on the board,
the child cannot say, and Milo asks again.
