# M-12 · step 05 · the run is incomplete

**The model became unavailable partway through and the service did what it is
built to do: the bank answered, and the child would have seen words.** The run
is evidence of that and is not a measurement of placing.

| | |
|---|---|
| calls | 70 |
| from the model | **56** |
| **from the bank, byte for byte** | **14** |
| first bank reply | `06_midway`·2 |
| a probe after the run | also the bank |

The break is not transient. A single call made after the run returned the bank
as well, so the key or the account behind it stopped working at
`06_midway`·2 and has not recovered.

## What that costs, precisely

**The two descriptions X6 exists to measure are entirely bank: 0 of 10
usable.** `no_vocabulary` and `no_vocabulary_2` are the child who cannot
describe the board, and whether Milo teaches or asks again cannot be read from
a fallback that never reads the child's message at all.

**Twelve descriptions are complete** — `b1` through `b7`, `chart_card_filled`,
`mounted_on_door`, `broken_on_purpose`, `01_midway` — and `06_midway` has one
model reply of five.

## What is not being done

**The fifty-six are not scored against a seventy-call baseline.** The
prediction is about *did not place* falling from 70 of 70 and *over-precise*
rising from nought, and both are counts over the whole fixture. A partial
count read against a whole one is a different measurement wearing the same
number.

**And nothing is re-run before the key is fixed.** A second run stitched to
the first would have two different services in one file.

## What this is evidence of

**V5, unrehearsed.** The failure drill cut the key deliberately in M-10 and
proved the bank answers; this is the same thing happening by itself, mid-run,
with nobody watching. It behaved exactly as the drill said it would — no
silence, no error to the caller, no half-reply. **56 model replies and 14 bank
replies in one file, and the only way to tell them apart is that the bank is
byte-identical to the corpus.**

Which is also the reason this was caught: `bank(ctx, lvl)` never reads the
child's message, so its replies are identical to the stage instruction and to
each other. **The property recorded in M-11 step 05 as a defect-shaped
non-defect is what made a broken run visible.**
