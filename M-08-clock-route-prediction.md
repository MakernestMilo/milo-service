# The clock-route block — prediction, before it is served

Committed before the block is landed and before any call is made.

## The block

Chapter-scoped to 11, served on the **clock route only** — never where the child
asked. Third attempt at this rung, after the rung label and the override line.

> NOBODY HAS ASKED YOU FOR ANYTHING
> You are here because time passed, not because they said anything…

## The architect's prediction

**L3-by-clock falls from 6/10 by at least two draws.** In tenths that is 4/10 or
lower carrying and 5/10 not carrying; this run is a five, so at n=5:

| | |
|---|---|
| baseline, line absent | **6/10 = 60%** |
| **carries** | **≤ 2/5 (≤ 40%)** — a fall of 20 points or more |
| does not carry | 3/5 (60%) — no movement |

**L2-by-clock should fall too**, from 3/10 = 30%. Recorded as the weaker call:
the block was written against L3's failure, not L2's.

**The ask controls must not move.** 0% at L3-ask and 40% at L4-ask. **If either
moves, the block has leaked past its scope**, and the run says nothing about the
block until the leak is fixed.

## What falsifies it

No movement at L3-by-clock, or movement in the wrong direction — which is what
the override line produced. Either way **the block comes out**.

## And what that would mean, decided now rather than after the number

Three attempts at this rung: a rung label, an instruction line, and a paragraph
of prose. If prose fails too, the finding is **that prose does not reach this
route**, and the honest next move is **different material rather than a fourth
block**.

Written before the run so it is not decided after seeing it.

## One engineering call, stated because it is not the architect's

The block says *"You are here because time passed"*. That is false at a cold
L0, where no failure has been seen and no clock is running. So it is served when
the chapter is 11, the child has not asked, **and a clock is actually running** —
which is L0-with-a-clock through L3-by-clock, and not L0-cold.

Consequence for this run: **11/L0 in the plan is cold, so it is untreated** and
should not move. It joins L3-ask and L4-ask as a control rather than a treated
position.
