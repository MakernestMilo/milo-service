Everything your machine can do

For chapter 12 and the Gift. Tick what your idea needs. That is your build list.

SENSE

Turn something real into a number I can read.

DECIDE

Compare that number with a line I chose.

ACT

Say it with light, or noise, or both.

STOP

End on its own, with a gap so it doesn't chatter.

COMPARE

Watch two places and notice the difference.

COUNT

Count events, not measure amounts.

REMEMBER

Keep a day or a week, with the time, and show it as a shape.

SEQUENCE

Do several things in an order, with gaps.

REACH

Make something happen in another room.

PLACE

Work where the problem is, not where the plug is.

DRESS

Have a body that doesn't stop it working.

SURVIVE

Be fixable by somebody who didn't build it.

If your idea needs something that isn't on this list, it doesn't mean you can't have it. It means you will have to work out how. Ask me at (makernest.com/origins/12).

Is it supposed to be doing that?

Most of the alarming things this machine does are on purpose.

WHAT IT IS DOING
WHICH IS IT?
The computer can't find the board
Install the USB driver at makernest.com/origins. Use the cable from the box.
It restarts when you plug in USB
Normal. Your settings and the notebook are kept.
Nothing lights up on battery
The pack's switch is off, or the pack is flat. A grown-up charges it.
The number never changes
On purpose in chapter 01. Otherwise the yellow wire is loose.
It says too hot when it isn't
On purpose in chapter 02. Otherwise check your line on card 02.
The buzzer won't stop
On purpose in chapter 03. Otherwise the output is set to ALWAYS.
The alarm flicks on and off
On purpose in chapter 04. Otherwise widen the gap.
Two sensors disagree
Normal, always. Half a degree is what measuring is like.
One opening counts twice
On purpose in chapter 06. Otherwise move the magnet closer and raise SETTLE.
There is a gap in the week
It lost power. Use a phone charger, not a computer.
The time is wrong
It lost power. The numbers are kept. Upload sketch 07 again.
Something is scorched
Real, and not your fault. Switch off. Email support@makernest.com.
THE RESET BUTTON ON THE SHIELD

Always allowed. Press it: the machine restarts. Hold the dial and press it: every setting goes back to how it came. It never erases the notebook, the strip or your cards.
