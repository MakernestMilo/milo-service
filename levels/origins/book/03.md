The one idea

An output is not decoration. It is how a decision gets from the machine to a person, and different decisions deserve different amounts of attention.

A light says: here is how things are, look when you like. A noise says: stop what you are doing. Choosing which one to use, and when, is a real engineering decision. You are about to make it.

What should it do, and who needs to notice?
MY ANSWER

05
BREAK IT ON PURPOSE · 14 MIN
The output with nothing behind it

On the WHEN COLD screen there is a choice called ALWAYS. Choose it.

The buzzer starts and does not stop. Give it fifteen seconds. Somebody in your house will say something.

Your machine still has a sensor. It still has your number. It has simply stopped asking.

A noise that is always on tells you nothing at all.

It can't mean "it's cold", because it also means "it's warm". Something that means everything means nothing.

Set it back. Notice how much more useful the buzzer is now that it is quiet most of the time.
