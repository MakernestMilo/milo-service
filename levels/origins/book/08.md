Build

Your machine is still recording chapter 07's week. Build this one where it stands. It keeps its notebook through everything you do today.

In compartment 08: a small switch board, the lamp on a metre of lead, and a 9 V battery with its clip.

01

Upload sketch 08 Chain Reaction. Bring the computer to the machine. Move the USB cable from the phone charger to the computer, upload, then move it straight back to the charger. The week carries on. You will see a gap of a minute or two on your chart. That gap was you.

02

Screw the switch board onto two spacers in the space marked LAMP on your sticker.

03

Three wires from the switch board's GND, VCC and SIG pins, down through the slot beside it, up beside the shield, onto row 9: black on G, red on V, grey on S.

04

Bring the lamp lead in at the back edge through the second pair of small slots. Cable tie, pulled snug. Screw its two wires into the switch board's V+ and V− terminals, marked LAMP on your sticker.

05

Stick the 9 V battery down in the space marked 9 V with a sticky pad from compartment 06. Clip it on and screw its wires into the VIN and GND terminals. The lamp gets its power from the 9 V. The machine just tells the switch board when.

05
BREAK IT ON PURPOSE · 14 MIN
A sequence never checks

Start the sequence. While it is running, pull the clip off the 9 V battery.

Steps 1 and 2 run. Step 3 does nothing at all. And the display says the sequence finished, perfectly happy with itself.

A sequence has no idea whether a step worked. It only knows what comes next. It sends each instruction out, and nothing comes back to say it worked.

Clip the battery back on. Now the second one. Put a 30 second gap before step 1 and run it. For half a minute your machine looks completely dead. You can't tell the difference between a machine that is waiting and a machine that has stopped.

Which is why, when you set it back, you will probably put the ring first. Something has to tell you it has started.

FOR THE GROWN-UP

The dead battery is the point of this chapter, so resist explaining it. A maker who says "it lied to me" has understood more than one who has been told about acknowledgements. The 9 V battery is not rechargeable. Don't put it on a charger.
