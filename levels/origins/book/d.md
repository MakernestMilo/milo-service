CHECK YOURSELF

The count has been read out to your family, and one thing has been changed because a real week annoyed a real person. A Doorkeeper that went up and came down the same evening hasn't been done, whatever anybody says.

That is act two. Your machine has a job, and other people have opinions about it.

FOR THE GROWN-UP

Your one job across this fortnight is keeping the three sessions apart. Makers will want to squash it into one heroic evening, and the week is the whole content. When they ask permission in session two, treat it as a real request with a real answer. A negotiated yes is worth more to this flagship than the electronics.
