The one idea

Two honest instruments in two places give two different answers, and neither of them is lying.

There is no such thing as "the temperature of the room". There is only the temperature somewhere. A number is always a number from somewhere. If you don't know where, you don't really know the number.

Which one of them is telling the truth?

That question has no correct answer. I put it here on purpose.

05
BREAK IT ON PURPOSE · 12 MIN
It is watching the wrong one

Your cold alarm from chapter 03 is still running underneath all this, and it is watching sensor A.

Take sensor B to the coldest place you found on the hunt. Look at B's number, and use it to set your cold alarm.

Nothing happens. Or worse, it goes off at completely the wrong moment.

The machine is not confused. You are. You are reading one place and it is watching another. There is nothing to fix here except a decision. On the dial, the ALARM WATCHES screen lets you choose A or B. Choose, and be able to say why.

Now the quiet half, which matters more than it looks. Put the two sensors back together, touching, and read them again. They still don't agree exactly. Two identical parts, in the same place, at the same moment, half a degree apart.

Every number has a little wobble in it. That isn't a fault. It is what measuring is like. It is why your alarm chattered in chapter 04.
