A body is a set of holes.

Everything your machine does has to survive being covered up: seeing, hearing, being heard, being read, being reached, being switched on. Working out what to cut a hole for is the engineering. The rest is decoration, and the decoration is entirely up to you.

BREAK IT ON PURPOSE · 15 MIN
The sealed box

One, the big one. Sensor A is shut inside a box with a board that gets slightly warm. Now it is measuring the inside of its own body. The number climbs and stops describing the room at all. Your machine can't see any more.

Two. The display is covered. The number still exists and nobody can read it.

Three. The reset button on the shield can't be reached. You will need it in chapter 11.

Four. The battery switch is shut in too, so you can't wake it or put it to sleep.

Five. The buzzer is muffled to almost nothing, or a cable is pinched where the card folds.

Cut the holes. The order you cut them in tells you what you think matters most, so notice what you cut first.

A body that stops the machine working isn't a body. It's a box.

FOR THE GROWN-UP

Don't suggest what the creature should look like, and don't tidy up the making. The value of this chapter is that the object stops being ours and becomes theirs. A grown-up's improvements undo that instantly.
