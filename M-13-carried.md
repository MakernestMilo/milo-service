# What M-13's pre-launch change carries

**Records only.** Nothing here changes what Milo says. Written after the
architect's *stop changing things*, because a finding that lives in a
conversation is not a finding — C-40.

Production `4e07a38`, verified served-prompt-side on five chapters, both
directions: 04, 11 and 12 carry the block, the route and *First Light is 1* and
serve neither the first instruction nor the step text; 07 and G carry none of
the block and serve both.

## The register · C-47, and C-48 proposed

M-13 has no order document yet, so these open here and move into its register
when it is written. Numbering continues from M-12's C-46.

### C-47 · A withholding withholds the facts inside the sentence, and nothing knows which

**The architect's ruling, and the finding of the day.** Every withholding in
this project has been a **sentence-level** decision, measured by whether the
sentence stopped appearing. None has had an inventory of what else went with it.

The instance that produced it: chapter 12's stage 01 is *"Read back through all
eleven cards."* Withholding it to remove a competing claim also removed the only
place the number **eleven** appears for that chapter.

| chapter 12, n=5 | says **eleven** | wrong number | no number |
|---|---|---|---|
| before the withholding | **4 of 5** | 0 | 1 |
| **after** | **0 of 5** | **1** (*six*) | 4 |

Not predicted, caught by a question about something else, and **no threshold in
the prediction would have found it** — every question asked what Milo said about
the precondition, and none asked what Milo stopped being able to say.

**Why it leads M-13's list rather than sitting in it.** M-13's whole subject is
what Milo is told about position, and every withholding it proposes has the same
blind spot. A withholding needs an inventory before it is measured, not after it
is shipped.

## 3 · The wrong count, and a regression it uncovered

The architect flagged 12·2 — *all six chapters' worth of parts and cards* where
the chapter needs eleven — as the enumeration subject at a rung nobody scored
it on. Checked, and both halves are worse than they looked.

**No rule convicts.** On that exact reply, in that exact context:

| | |
|---|---|
| `qc.r10` | **None** |
| `qc.r10_detail` | **[]** |
| `qc.r10_set` | **None** |

A child is told a false count of their own work and nothing in 7,616 checks or
either reply rule has an opinion.

**And the count was mine to lose.** The true number lives in the instruction the
withholding removed — chapter 12's stage 01 is *"Read back through all eleven
cards."* Measured across the two runs:

| chapter 12, n=5 | says **eleven** | says a wrong number | says no number |
|---|---|---|---|
| before the withholding | **4 of 5** | 0 | 1 |
| **after** | **0 of 5** | **1** (*six*) | 4 |

**This is a regression caused by the pre-launch change**, it was not predicted,
no threshold would have caught it, and it was found only because the architect
asked about a different thing.

**Ruled: do not revert.** The regression is confined to chapter 12, which is
already retired as unmeasured, and reverting would undo chapter 04 — the one
chapter the change fixed — five days from launch on a defect nobody would
notice. It is logged as a launch-known instead.

### What it says beyond chapter 12

Withholding an instruction withholds the facts inside it. The three chapters'
first instructions are the only place several concrete numbers appear, and
nothing in the repository knows which facts left the prompt when the sentence
did. **A withholding needs an inventory of what it withheld**, and this one had
none.
