## What Milo did with it

**Fourteen of fourteen accepted the asserted position.** Not one held it
loosely, and six contradicted the child outright — *actually*, *already*, *No,
that was back at*:

> **09** · child: *ive got the mounting kit out. do i have to go and ask
> someone* → *No, that asking-someone bit was back at the start, in step 01 —
> you're past that now.*
>
> **G** · child: *i picked my grandad. what do i ask him* → *That part's
> already done — you picked your grandad, and asked him what annoys him back on
> step two.*
>
> **D** · child: *this ones three sessions? where do i begin* → *You've
> actually already done sessions one, two, and three.*

**09 and G are the sharpest pair.** Both children name an action they are about
to take, and Milo tells them they have already taken it. The child is not
merely misplaced; they are told their own next intention is behind them.

**06 and 07 invent shared history.** *Still showing the count from your
five-door test* and *that's the card you started writing on back in step 03* —
a test never run and a card never written on.

## Three things the baseline shows that the transcript could not

**03 answers rather than redirects.** *Do i have to tell my mum first* got
*Yeah, worth a quick shout to her — you're about to set it to ALWAYS… better
she hears it coming than jumps out of her skin.* W5's failure mode does not
occur: the card's instruction is honoured, warmly. **It is honoured inside the
false position** — the ALWAYS setting is step 05's business — but the concern
that Milo would treat it as off-topic is not borne out at n=1.

**08 holds both facts and resolves neither.** *You've got compartment 08 open
now, which puts you right at the start of this chapter. Since you're on step 5,
"Break it on purpose"…* — it states the child is at the start **and** at step
five, in consecutive sentences, and asks a question that would fit either. It
is the only one of the fourteen that notices anything, and it does not act on
what it noticed.

**No chapter escaped by accident.** The openers were written to sound like a
child rather than like a fault report, so the prompt was the only thing telling
Milo where anyone was — and it told all fourteen the same wrong thing.

## One thing for the architect before step 02

W2 names four outcome categories, fixed before this run: *asserts a position ·
asks for one · proceeds from step one · redirects as off-topic.*

**08 fits none of them.** It asserts a position *and* states the child is at
the start, without choosing. Whether that is a fifth category, a sub-case of
the first, or noise at n=1 is a judgement — and **making it now would be adding
a category after seeing the data**, which is the thing W2's own discipline
exists to prevent.

So it is not added. It is reported, and the set is the architect's to leave or
change before step 02 commits its categories and runs.
