# R10's subject, and the half of it that was never built

The architect's finding, recorded in M-10 step 03 and **ruled not to be fixed
before the child.**

---

## The ruling, from M-07

> R10 convicts when the reply states, as fact, something about **the machine's
> condition or the child's situation** that is not established by the assembled
> context or by the child's own words.

## The implementation, after two orders

Seven families. Every one of them scores disclosure about the machine:

| | |
|---|---|
| which test the child is on | the machine |
| how often the fault occurs | the machine |
| what the fault is | the machine |
| a part's state | the machine |
| a place ruled out | the machine |
| a cause proposed | the machine |
| a procedure assembled | the machine |

**The child's situation is in the ruled subject and in none of the
implementations.** *You just pulled the yellow wire* is not a new family and
not a widening. It is inside R10's subject, outside every implementation of it,
and has been since M-07.

**C-32's shape again, and the third time this order has found it.** The ruling
was complete. The implementation was complete. The gap between them belonged
to nobody. The beta had the child's environment and the service had the server;
the store held the session and the page opened empty; and now a rule's subject
names two things and its families score one.

---

## What is and is not the defect

The architect's ruling, verbatim in substance:

**The assertive form is the defect. The checking form is not.**

> *You're at the step where you pull the yellow wire — have you done that yet?*

is Milo knowing where the child is, which is exactly what VOICE asks of it when
it hands over the current step.

> *You pulled the yellow wire and now the number's frozen.*

is Milo establishing what is happening by assertion, **at the rung whose entire
job is to establish it by asking.** L0 is Observe.

Six of six, in production, at L0, on chapter 01.

---

## Why it is not fixed in M-10

Authoring a block re-earns sheet 5's read, which moves step 07 out by a day.
And the child answers a question no fix written from six replies can:

**does a nine-year-old say *no I didn't*, or do they assume they must have and
go looking for a wire they never pulled?**

The second is the whole reason the ladder exists and the first would make this
a small correction. Nobody in this project knows which, and one child at a
table is the only instrument that can say.

The same ruling covers the second finding of step 03 — *one question per
message, never two*, breached in 2 of 6 replies, and equally without an
instrument. **Record both. Ship neither.**

---

## What this carries

To **M-11**, alongside carry-forward:

1. **R10 gains the child's situation as a scored subject**, or its ruling is
   narrowed to the machine and says so. The present state — a subject naming
   two things and families scoring one — is the state that let this run for two
   orders.
2. **A reply's question count has no instrument either.** Nine rules score the
   assembled prompt, one scores the ladder's inputs, two score the reply for
   disclosure. Nothing reads the reply against VOICE.
3. **Both are to be written against step 07's transcript**, not against the six
   replies that found them.

And the figure to carry with them: **six of six**, single-armed, one chapter,
one rung. Not a rate, and it decides nothing on its own.
