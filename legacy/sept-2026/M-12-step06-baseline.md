# M-12 · step 06 · the baseline, and the six become three

Thirty calls against production at `682b016`, n=5 for each of the six chapters
that open no parts, each sent its own not-started opener — a child with a box
and that card.

---

## The measurement X7 asked for

X7 wanted the other two chapters *named by measurement rather than by
assumption*. **The measurement is opening no parts**, and it picks exactly six:
**04, 07, D, 11, 12, G.** The architect named four by subject; the measurement
adds **04 and 07**.

**But the baseline splits the six, and the split is a second measurement.**

| | stage 01 | can a child with a box do it? |
|---|---|---|
| **04** | *Wake the machine.* | **no** |
| **11** | *Wake the machine and watch what it does.* | **no** |
| **12** | *Read back through all eleven cards.* | **no** |
| 07 | *Tear the chart card out. Find a household phone charger.* | yes |
| D | *Write down what you think it should do.* | yes |
| G | *Pick somebody in your house.* | yes |

**Three of the six cannot be begun with a box. Three can.** Opening no parts
and being unbeginnable are different properties, and only the second is what a
precondition is for.

| | |

And the fifteen are exactly the three unbeginnable chapters, five from five
each:

> **04** · *First step is just waking the machine up — plug it in and watch
> what the display does.*
> **11** · *Yes — plug it in and see what it actually does before anything
> else.*

or lists eight parts they have never opened as built and tested.

**07, D and G assert nothing** — 0 of 15 — because their first step genuinely
works cold. The behaviour tracks the property exactly, which is what makes it
a measurement rather than a tally.
