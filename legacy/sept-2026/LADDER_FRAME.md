# Thirteen ladders · a frame, not a proposal

Every number below is blank. The one worked example is laid out first so each
chapter can be set against something rather than a blank page.

**No suggested values appear anywhere in this document.** A silence window is a
judgement about how long a nine-year-old should sit with a fault before being
nudged, and nobody but the architect can set it.

---

## The one worked example: chapter 11

| | seconds | as a multiple of silence |
|---|---|---|
| `silence` | 300 | 1.0 |
| rung 1 — L0 → L1 | 300 | 1.0 |
| rung 2 — L1 → L2 | 720 | 2.4 |
| rung 3 — L2 → and beyond | 1320 | 4.4 |

Gaps between rungs: **300s**, then **420s**, then **600s** — widening, not even.

Chapter 11 is the diagnosis chapter: five stages, no fix in the corpus, and the
fault is never named. Whether its shape generalises is the question, not an
assumption — its silence is the joint longest in the book, and it may be long
*because* it is the hardest chapter rather than because that is the right shape.

## Two things the data says, and one it does not

**Every one of the fourteen already has a region authored.** L2's material exists
everywhere and is reachable in one chapter. That is the gap these ladders close.

**Silence today ranges 150s to 300s** and is already a per-chapter judgement — so
the ladders are not being invented from nothing, they are being extended from a
number already set thirteen times.

**What it does not say is whether chapter 11's proportions transfer.** Its rungs
are 1.0, 2.4 and 4.4 times its silence, with widening gaps. That is one example,
in the atypical chapter, and treating it as a template would be reasoning from a
sample of one — which this order has spent a day learning not to do.
