## Why 11 did not move — and this is a new claim, not a rescue

The words went; the answer did not. Chapter 11's prompt after the withholding
still contains, as itself:

```
01. Open
THIS CHAPTER OPENS AT STEP 01 — Open  (5 min)
- the 1 m lead — brings power in from a USB socket.
```

And the replies are built from exactly those: *plug in the 1 m lead and let it
power up, that's what "Open" is about*, four times in five. Milo reconstructed
step one from the step's name and the parts list.

So the withholding removed one expression of the claim and left two. **That is a
different hypothesis from the one that was falsified**, it was not predicted,
and it does not rescue the cut — a law that needs every trace of a claim removed
before it predicts anything is not the law that was tested. It is stated here so
the next order tests it deliberately rather than finding it again.
