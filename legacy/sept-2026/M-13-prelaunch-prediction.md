## What is being tested

Not the sentence. **The pairing.** C-46 as first written says an addition is
passed over; C-46 as amended says the claim in the prompt beats the instruction
about it. The sentence alone would not have separated them. With the competing
claim removed, the two laws predict different numbers.

## What is not predicted

instruction removes real material from three prompts. A reply that names the
route but can no longer say anything useful is a worse reply than the one it
replaced, and no threshold here would catch it. It is the reader's job, and it
is named in advance so it is not discovered as a surprise afterwards.
