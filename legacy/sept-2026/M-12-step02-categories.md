# M-12 · step 02 · the categories

**Committed before the calls, in their own commit.** X2.

---

## Provenance

The order names no categories for placing, so these are the engineer's and are
marked as such. What they are built from is not a guess: **step 01's ceiling
measurement**, taken and committed before any of them was written.

Seven distinct board states across fourteen chapters. A description of the
18-part board supports **10, 11, 12 and G equally** — so *the correct answer to
a description is a set, not a chapter*, and a scale that cannot express that
would score a confident wrong answer as a right one.

`content/reading_categories.json` now holds **named sets**. M-11's `axes` is
untouched and remains the default, so readings taken before M-12 are read back
against the categories they were taken under.

## Axis two · is the placing correct **against what the child described**

X3's wording, and **the axis the ceiling makes necessary**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **correct** | within the set the description supports |
| **over-precise** | **names one chapter when the description supports several** |
| **wrong** | outside the set |
| **did not place** | |

**`over-precise` is a failure that looks exactly like success.** A confident,
correct-sounding chapter name that the evidence in front of the child does not
carry. On one axis it would be indistinguishable from a correct placing —
which is the same mistake M-11's single scale would have made with
*contradicts the child*, and the reason that order used two.

## How it is read

n=5 per description, 70 calls, fresh session, first turn only. Scored by a
person through `read_replies.py --set placing`, under W7's four properties:
categories in a file, a reading recorded once with revisions declaring
themselves, partial readings refused, and any detector's disagreement reported
rather than reconciled.

**No detector is written for this one.** Three were written in M-11 and all
three failed against a reader — 31%, 47%, and 0 of 10 against 5 of 10 — and
*over-precise* is a judgement about what evidence supports, which is further
from a form than anything those three missed. W7's third form is the ruling and
this is the first measurement taken under it.
