# The Origins bench pack

The book promises a bench experiment for every chapter. The BOM of 22 October 2026 has a
breadboard, 5 red LEDs, 5 × 10 kΩ, 5 × 100 nF, 5 BC546 transistors and jumpers, which is not
enough to build the experiments as their titles are printed. These parts are added:

| Part | Qty | Used in bench |
|---|---|---|
| Light sensor, LDR GL5528, 5 mm | 2 | 02, 04, 05 (both), 07, D, 10 |
| Push button, 6 × 6 mm, four legs | 2 | 06 (both), D |
| Yellow LED, 5 mm | 1 | 08 |
| Green LED, 5 mm | 1 | 08 |
| Resistor 220 Ω, 1/4 W | 10 | 01 (two), 02, 04, 05, 08 (three), 09 |
| Passive buzzer board, KY-006 (piezo type only: a 16 Ω magnetic buzzer would draw too much from the pin) | 1 | 03, D |
| SG90 micro servo, with horns | 1 | 10 |
| Soil moisture sensor, YL-69 probe and comparator board | 1 | 09 |

Already in the box and used: the breadboard, red LEDs, 10 kΩ resistors (LDR dividers), the 9 V
battery and clip (bench 01), male-to-female and female-to-female jumpers.

In the box and not used by any bench experiment yet: the 100 nF capacitors and the BC546
transistors. They are listed on the parts page so Milo knows them.

Every bench circuit uses only the shield's free rows: 10, 12, 13, A0 and A1. Nothing on the
machine is unplugged, and no bench sketch touches the saved settings or the notebook.
